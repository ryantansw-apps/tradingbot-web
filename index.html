Baskets (your mini fund) PRACTICE ▾

Pick stocks and how much of your money goes into each. "Add a stock" lists your Research list first, then the other most-traded stocks. One tap queues an order for every stock. You choose the stocks; this is a tool, not advice.
Each stock becomes its own limit order, good for today, priced a touch above the last price so it is more likely to fill. Shares are whole numbers, so a little cash is left over. Each order pays its own minimum fee, so very small amounts lose more to fees.
